Pinball CTL is very much a work in progress. It is not a stable release, and it is not ready to be treated as a finished, production-ready controller. We welcome builders who want to explore, test and help improve it.

[[art:information:0]]

What that means for your build

Features, configuration formats and behaviour are still evolving. You may encounter bugs, incomplete workflows or changes between revisions. A feature working on one development machine does not establish that it works reliably with every hardware combination.

The website and manual follow ongoing development. A version number or download does not mean the project has reached a stable release. Check the exact code revision, firmware compatibility and known limitations before trying an update, and keep backups of your project.

Your feedback makes a difference

Tell us what worked, what was confusing and where you got stuck. Reports from real builds, careful testing and improvements to the documentation all help make Pinball CTL better.

GitHub is the place to report problems, share ideas, compare results and contribute. You do not need to write code to help: a clear description of a reproducible problem or an unclear instruction is valuable.

Test carefully, one step at a time

Start with inputs and non-coil actions, with high-current output power disconnected. Software previews and passing tests do not certify your wiring, driver circuits or coil safety. Follow the Hardware guide and verify behaviour on your actual installation before powered use.

Keep passwords, tokens, Wi-Fi credentials and personal information out of public reports, logs and screenshots. For more reporting guidance, visit Contact & Support.
