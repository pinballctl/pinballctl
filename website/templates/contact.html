Help us understand the problem.

A useful bug report gives enough context to reproduce the behaviour. Include:

  • The pinballCTL and firmware versions, host operating system and controller board.
  • The steps you took, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Relevant screenshots and a short log excerpt from the time of the problem.
  • Whether the issue happens every time or only under particular conditions.

Remove passwords, Wi-Fi credentials, tokens and personal information before sharing logs or project files. GitHub issues are public. Do not post security-sensitive details in an issue.
