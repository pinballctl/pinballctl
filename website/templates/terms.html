Working with a physical machine

pinballCTL is an evolving homebrew pinball control platform. Hardware compatibility and behaviour depend on the board, firmware, wiring, drivers and configuration you choose.

You are responsible for selecting suitable components, power supplies, fusing and output drivers; validating wiring and limits; and testing changes before normal operation. Firmware safety features are one part of a complete machine design. They do not replace physical isolation, appropriate emergency-stop arrangements or safe maintenance procedures.

Examples, screenshots and default settings demonstrate software behaviour. They are not specifications for every coil or machine. Back up your project and review release notes before upgrading.
