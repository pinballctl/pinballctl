Who and what this covers

This notice describes the public pinballCTL website and its documentation at www.pinballctl.com. The contact page lists the project’s available contact channels. A private address for privacy enquiries is not currently published.

The website does not offer user accounts, a checkout or a contact form. Your separately installed pinballCTL application runs on a host you control; its data is discussed below.

Website hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See GitHub’s Pages documentation and privacy statement for its handling of visitor information.

Google Analytics

The live website uses Google Analytics to understand which pages and tools visitors find useful. It measures page views and interactions such as opening search, choosing a search result, following selected links and enlarging screenshots. Google may also process browser, device and referral information as part of the service.

The site starts analytics by default unless this browser has a saved opt-out. Its custom search events record the selected result or that search was opened; they do not include your typed search query. Site search itself runs in your browser against a static index.

Analytics uses Google’s services and may set cookies such as _ga to distinguish visits. Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from partner sites for more detail about its processing, including processing across countries.

Your browser choices

Open Cookie settings and choose “Turn analytics off” to stop subsequent analytics collection through this site’s integration. The site also attempts to remove accessible Google Analytics cookies. This does not retract information already sent or remove hosting security logs.

Your choice is stored in local storage under pinballctl.analytics. It stays in that browser until changed or cleared. A different browser or device has its own choice. Clearing site storage removes the saved preference, so the default applies again on your next visit. Browser privacy tools may provide additional controls.

When you contact the project

If you email the maintainer or open an issue, the message and any details you provide are used to understand and respond to your enquiry. Include only what is needed. Public GitHub issues, comments and attachments are visible to others and are also subject to GitHub’s policies.

Do not post passwords, tokens, Wi-Fi credentials or sensitive personal information in a public issue. Use the email address above for private enquiries, and remove unnecessary details from logs before sharing them.

Your local pinballCTL installation

Machine configuration, rules, logs and media are maintained on your installation’s host. Visiting this website does not give it access to your local machine files. Downloading updates, using external integrations or choosing to share an export or log can involve other services.

You control your host, network, backups and exports. Protect administrator credentials and review files before uploading them to a support request. The website’s analytics setting applies to this website, not to every service you may use alongside your machine.

Retention, requests and updates

Browser preferences remain until you change or clear them. Hosting data is handled under GitHub’s policies; analytics retention depends on the active Google Analytics property settings. If you need details of retention for information you have shared with the project, contact the maintainer.

Depending on applicable law and the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information, or to object to its use. Check the contact page for available channels; do not post personal information or identity documents in public issues. You may also raise concerns with your local data-protection authority, including the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
