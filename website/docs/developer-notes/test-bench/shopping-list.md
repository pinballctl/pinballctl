### Components taken from the electronics kit

P21 supplies the single **70 × 50 mm board**, **10 × 4.7 kΩ**, **3 × 10 kΩ**, **1 × 330 Ω**, **1 × 100 nF** and **2 × 470 µF / 16 V**. P19/P20 supply the two interface devices. The [single-board assembly schedule](interface-board.md#board-layout-and-fitted-parts) gives their locations, fit limits and wiring. Eight optical inputs use ESP internal pull-ups; no external sensor circuit is fitted. Main LED power stays in the external 18 AWG/WAGO distribution.

### Sourcing decisions and remaining checks

The 21 November review replaced the unavailable washer kit and slow ball/buffer offers with matching nominal sizes or the same IC type. The five-ball pack supplies the three fitted balls; the ten-chip pack supplies one buffer. Standard MDF preserves the design thicknesses and main cut sizes while arriving earlier than the original MR sheets. Both MDF thicknesses now use **PARTY DECOR (TM)**: two 6 mm A2 sheets and one 12 mm A2 sheet cost **£35.24**, saving **£18.24** against the Harlow combination (£53.48), with displayed delivery on 24 November instead of 25–27 November. A2 fits the 370 mm carrier and 360 mm course on separate sheets; the 12 mm wall/rail cuts also fit within A2 with a 3 mm kerf. These choices update purchasing, not physical validation: all [fit and electrical checks](checks.md) still apply.

The Adafruit sensors remain selected because their open-collector output is documented for the existing ESP pull-up circuit. The faster Treedix listing specifies 20 × 10 × 9 mm heads, a 5 mm optical opening, 300 mm leads and NPN normally-open output, but lacks a clear output schematic; its current figures are also inconsistent. Measure an evaluation pair before changing the [sensor wiring](wiring.md#direct-sensor-connections) or rail apertures. The faster GeeekPi power board needs its own mounting and cooling review; it has not replaced P24 in the drawings or subtotal. The exact box is retained to avoid changing every fitted panel around an unverified cavity.
