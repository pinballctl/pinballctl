# Test Bench: Assembly overview

The bench has a removable carrier, an L-shaped ball course and a console above the electronics. The [layout plan](../test-bench.md#layout-and-illustrations) shows their arrangement in the box.

## Carrier and retaining cleats

![Carrier and retaining cleats with an assembly view.](./media/build-steps-carrier.png)

The carrier supports the course and electronics. Four sidewall cleats retain it while allowing the complete assembly to lift out of the box.

## Console

![Console top and supporting U-frame in an exploded assembly view.](./media/build-steps-console.png)

A U-shaped frame supports the removable console top. Its open front provides access and ventilation. The LCD, buttons, lamps and interface board are carried by the top; the Pi and power electronics sit below it.

## Ball course

![L-shaped course, rails and end stops.](./media/build-steps-course.png)

The course follows the rear and left sides of the box. Rails form a **30 mm clear lane** for the 27 mm balls, with end stops at the shooter pocket and trough. The installed course slopes approximately **2°** toward the trough.

## Course supports and sensors

![Course support pads, shims and sensor pads.](./media/build-steps-supports.png)

Pads and shims support the sloping course. Separate sensor pads align the emitter and receiver heads with apertures in the rails. Eight break-beam pairs report ball positions and passage to pinballctl through the [sensor inputs](wiring.md#direct-sensor-connections).

## Gate and transport cover

![Manual gate, guides, trough cover and keeper.](./media/build-steps-gate.png)

The manual gate holds the ball at the launch position and lifts **30 mm** to release it. A separate padded cover retains balls in the trough during transport and is removed for operation.

## Lid fittings

![Catch and lid-stay backing in an exploded mounting view.](./media/build-steps-closure.png)

Two front catches secure the lid. An external locking stay supports the open lid at the right-rear corner, with backing pieces spreading the loads on the thin lid panel.
