The open wooden 12 V bench

A tabletop patch bench for the 24 components in the saved machine map. Keep the Pi beside the board on its own supply. Its USB data cable powers the Freenove controller. One external 12 V DC feed powers the lamps and lighting converter.

Each 30-pixel row has its own fused positive and return pair, fed at both ends from that row’s branch. Keep the +5 V pads electrically separate between rows so one fuse cannot feed another branch. Join DOUT to the next DIN and carry the data reference ground alongside. Feed the 12- and 6-pixel groups independently. Put a capacitor across each of the three strip groups at its first input, negative stripe to ground.

Use the logic/status branch for LCD high side and AHCT power. Fit 100 nF directly between DIP pins 14 (+5 V) and 7 (GND). Ground active-low enables 1, 4 and 10. Tie unused enable 13 to +5 V and input 12 to ground. Output 11 stays open. Each used output gets 330 Ω in series at the first pixel. NeoPixel wiring guidance

Load budget

At 60 mA per RGB pixel, the rows draw up to 1.80 A each, table 0.72 A and status 0.36 A. Total LED allowance is 8.28 A at 5 V. Allow another 0.25 A for LCD and logic. At an assumed 85% conversion efficiency this is 4.18 A from 12 V, plus the two indicators. Verify measured input stays under 5 A and every branch stays within its fuse, contact and cable ratings. The 8.53 A full-white estimate exceeds the converter’s 8.1 A rating at 12 V input. Use reduced brightness and measure output current below the temperature-derated limit. Full-white testing requires a suitably uprated supply path. Pixel power estimates

USB supplies the ESP. Keep external +5 V off its VBUS, VIN and 5 V headers. Power the 12 V peripheral side before starting ESP output signals. Stop signals and disconnect USB before switching peripheral power off. Route all pixel current through the fused harness, clear of breadboard contacts and signal PCB tracks.

Verify both events independently. The saved mapping alone cannot establish a safe simultaneous 15-input pinout.

Bring-up sequence

1. Disconnect USB and loads. Check polarity and continuity. Energise converter alone and measure 5.0 V. Add one fused branch at a time. Check ESP-side I²C idle ≤3.3 V.

2. Connect host, check framed bridge discovery and reconnect. Exercise each contact once in diagnostics, then test scoring and backglass on the host. Write both LCD rows, check tilt axes, toggle both discrete lamps.

3. Chase first and last pixel on each channel. Increase brightness in steps while measuring input current, far-end voltage and connector temperature. Stop on flicker, excessive drop or heating.

4. Verify disabled outputs stay off. With lamps fitted, use an enabled lamp-only bench profile and firmware arming to scope a single 10 ms pulse on IN. Check minimum off-time (1 s), disarm and fault handling. A lamp shows switching only. Keep the machine profile and real coils disconnected.

Basis: local mapping of 10 November 2026 and ButtonDefault firmware. Select, Add Player and a fourth RGB channel belong to the earlier concept and have no current machine assignment. Physical fit, thermal performance and deployment await a bench build.
